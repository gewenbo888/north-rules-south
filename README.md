# North Rules South · 北治南

A bilingual (EN + 中文) intellectual deep-dive on the twelve-cell matrix of conquest dynasties: Northern peoples ruling Southern civilizations across four millennia of Old-World history, the two exceptions (Anglo-Saxons, Israel), and what comes next.

## Links

- **Live:** [north-rules-south.psyverse.fun](https://north-rules-south.psyverse.fun)
- **GitHub:** [github.com/gewenbo888/north-rules-south](https://github.com/gewenbo888/north-rules-south)

## Sections

1. **Matrix** — the original twelve-cell table, made interactive
2. **Thesis** — the pattern, the mechanism, the exceptions
3. **Cases** — eleven conquest regimes, one liberation order
4. **Map** — the geographic vector field of conquest
5. **Martial Virtue Indices** — heuristic rankings of North & South
6. **The Jewish Anomaly** — the five Jewish "fathers" of modern physics, computing, aerodynamics, and the bomb
7. **Timeline** — twelve centuries of the pattern
8. **What Comes Next** — China, Israel, the Anglo order
9. **Library** — Lattimore, Fletcher, Crossley, İnalcık, Richards, Ferguson, Marx, Mead
